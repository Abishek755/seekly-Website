Answers to Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our Hindi learning programs, DBHPS exam preparation, and AI-driven coaching.

What courses does Seekhly EdTech offer? expand_more
Seekhly provides three primary educational programs:
    DBHPS Exam Coaching: Complete preparation for all 8 levels of Dakshina Bharat Hindi Prachar Sabha exams (Prathamic, Madhyama, Rashtrabhasha, Praveshika, Visharad Poorvardh/Uttarardh, Praveen Poorvardh/Uttarardh). 8-Level Spoken Hindi Mastery: From absolute beginner to fluent speaker, powered by real-time AI phonetic analysis. Govt. Teacher Certification: Advanced pedagogy and syllabus training for aspiring Hindi teachers.
How does Seekhly help Tamil native speakers learn Hindi faster? expand_more
Seekhly utilizes a specialized bilingual methodology tailored for Tamil speakers. We explain Hindi grammar, sentence patterns, and vocabulary through Tamil and English parallels, combined with our AI voice platform that analyzes accent and phonetic precision.
Are DBHPS examination certificates officially recognized? expand_more
Yes. Certificates issued by the Dakshina Bharat Hindi Prachar Sabha (DBHPS) are nationally recognized by government bodies, educational boards, and public sector organizations across India.
Can I join online classes from anywhere in Tamil Nadu or Bangalore? expand_more
Absolutely! Headquartered in Kovilpatti, Seekhly offers live interactive online batches accessible to students in Kovilpatti, Chennai, Madurai, Coimbatore, Tirunelveli, Bangalore, and across India, with flexible timings and recording access.
How do I enroll and receive course fee details? expand_more
Simply click the "Join Now" button on our navigation bar or course sections, select your desired course/DBHPS level, and submit the inquiry form. Our team will send instant details and fee structures directly to your WhatsApp number.
